Just a Little Reminder

You are going to be fine.

Not because everything magically fixes itself — but because you're stronger than what you're carrying. You've gotten through every hard day you've ever had. All of them, one hundred percent. That wasn't luck. That wasn't chance. That was you.

And you're not behind. You're not doing too little. You're not relaxing too much or setting yourself back. You're just a person moving through a hard season — and seasons pass. They always do. The sun hasn't forgotten you. It's just behind the clouds for a moment.

You don't have to carry this alone.

I'm here. Not to fix you or rush you or tell you to cheer up — just to be here. To listen. To sit with you in it. To hold your hand while you find your way back to yourself.

When it gets too heavy, come to me. When you don't have the words, come to me. When all you want to do is be quiet, come to me — I'll be quiet with you. You don't have to be okay around me. You never have to perform for me. Not once.

We'll figure it out together. Whatever this is, whatever it takes, however long it takes. I'm not going anywhere.

You are loved. You are held. You are not alone. You are my blessing, motho waka, and I am in this with you — ke go vrostana blind, through every season.

One day at a time. One breath at a time. And I'll be right there with you for every single one.
